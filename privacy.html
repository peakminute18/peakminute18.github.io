Privacy policy

PEAK MINUTE poster · last updated November 25, 2026

PEAK MINUTE poster is a private tool used only by the owner of the PEAK MINUTE channels to upload short video clips to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not offered to anyone else and has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google's and Instagram's official APIs, it:

  • uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API, youtube.upload);
  • reads the owner's own channel and the view, like and comment counts of the videos it uploaded (youtube.readonly), to learn which clips do well;
  • publishes Reels to the owner's own Instagram account and reads their view counts.

It does not access any other person's account, contacts, email, files or personal data.

Where the data stays

The login tokens and the view counts are stored only on the owner's own computer at home. They are not sold, shared or given to anyone, and are not used for advertising.

Use of Google data

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The YouTube part is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in Instagram's settings under Apps and websites. Stored tokens are then deleted from the computer.
